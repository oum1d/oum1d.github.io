About

I am a web designer based in Białystok, Poland. I help small and growing businesses get a website that clearly shows why they are worth choosing — and brings in enquiries.

Not only simple pages. The portfolio on this site includes a shop with a catalogue and a cart, a booking system with real availability, a dispatcher dashboard with separate screens for different roles, and a property developer's site with a staff panel where the team updates prices themselves.

How a project runs

We start with questions, not with colours. What does the business sell, who is the visitor, and what should they do on the page? Half of a good site is decided before anything is drawn.

Structure, then design, then words. I plan the pages and the path through them, then the visual side, then the copy. If you do not have texts, I write them — clearly, in your language, without marketing noise.

Built and tested, not mocked up. You get a working site, not a picture of one. Before handover I check it on phones, tablets and laptops: speed, contrast, readability, forms, every link. You get a short report of what was done and what you can check yourself.

Published. I set up hosting and put the site online. You keep the accounts and the keys — I never hold your business hostage.

What I can build

Landing pages and multi-page business sites. Catalogues and shops with a cart. Booking and enquiry forms with spam protection. Admin panels where your team edits content without a developer. Sites in two or three languages — Polish, Ukrainian, English.

Honest limits

I am at the start of my freelance path, and everything in this portfolio is a concept project: the companies are invented, the tasks are real. Each project states this openly on the site itself. I do not invent clients, reviews or numbers — what you see here is what I actually built and measured.

If your project needs something I have not done before, I will say so rather than learn it on your budget without telling you.
