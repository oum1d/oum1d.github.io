that clearly shows why they are worth choosing — and brings in enquiries.

Not only simple pages. The portfolio on this site includes a shop with a catalogue and a cart, a
booking system with real availability, a dispatcher dashboard with separate screens for different
roles, and a property developer's site with a staff panel where the team updates prices themselves.

## How a project runs

**We start with questions, not with colours.** What does the business sell, who is the visitor, and
what should they do on the page? Half of a good site is decided before anything is drawn.

**Structure, then design, then words.** I plan the pages and the path through them, then the visual
side, then the copy. If you do not have texts, I write them — clearly, in your language, without
marketing noise.

**Built and tested, not mocked up.** You get a working site, not a picture of one. Before handover I
check it on phones, tablets and laptops: speed, contrast, readability, forms, every link. You get a
short report of what was done and what you can check yourself.

**Published.** I set up hosting and put the site online. You keep the accounts and the keys — I never
hold your business hostage.

## What I can build

Landing pages and multi-page business sites. Catalogues and shops with a cart. Booking and enquiry
