How I work

Before web design I wrote copy for businesses. So I do not start with pictures. I start with a question: what should a visitor understand, and what should they do next? The structure, the design and the words follow from the answer.

Every project here is built, not mocked up. Filters, carts, booking, admin panels and price histories actually work — you can open them and click. Before handing a site over I test it on phones and laptops for speed, contrast and readability, and write down what I checked.
